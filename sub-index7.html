Project Overview

This guard tower structure was designed in SolidWorks, featuring an elevated observation platform supported by a structural frame and staircase access system. The model was created to represent a realistic tower structure used for surveillance or monitoring purposes. Each component, from the support columns to the platform, railings and stairs was carefully modeled to demonstrate accurate structural geometry and assembly organization.


Key elements of the tower, including the support frame, platform enclosure, guard railings, and stair system, were developed using SolidWorks features such as Extrude, Cut-Extrude and Mirror to maintain consistent geometry and symmetry. Fillet and Chamfer tools were applied to refine edges and improve the overall visual quality of the structure. The final model was rendered in Blender using realistic lighting and camera composition to enhance the presentation and highlight the structural details. This project demonstrates skills in 3D CAD modeling, structural design representation and engineering visualization suitable for portfolios and design presentations.
